# 🍽️ Food Delivery Business Intelligence & Operational Performance Analytics

An end-to-end data analytics project focused on understanding food delivery business performance through Microsoft Fabric, SQL and Power BI.

The project transforms food delivery data into actionable business insights by analyzing revenue, restaurant performance, food categories, ratings, geographic trends and revenue concentration.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall food delivery revenue and order performance.
- Identify high-performing restaurants and popular food categories.
- Evaluate monthly sales trends and revenue growth.
- Understand city-wise and state-wise business performance.
- Analyze restaurant ratings and price segments.
- Identify revenue concentration and restaurant performance patterns.
- Generate actionable business insights for decision-making.

## 🔍 SQL Analysis

The SQL analysis covers the following areas:

1. Database and table exploration.
2. Data quality and missing-value validation.
3. Primary key and duplicate checks.
4. Referential integrity validation.
5. Date and dimension analysis.
6. Overall business KPI calculations.
7. Monthly revenue and growth analysis.
8. Restaurant performance ranking.
9. Dish and food category analysis.
10. City and state performance.
11. Restaurant rating analysis.
12. Price segmentation.
13. City-level revenue contribution.
14. Revenue quartile analysis.
15. Monthly restaurant performance.
16. Revenue volatility and stability.
17. High-revenue stable restaurant identification.
18. Pareto revenue contribution analysis.

- CASE expressions
- Window functions

- Statistical aggregation

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- Total revenue reached approximately ₹53.01 million across 197,430 orders.
- KFC was the highest revenue-generating restaurant, contributing approximately 8.01% of total revenue.
- The top 10 restaurants contributed approximately 34.16% of overall revenue.
- Approximately 25.28% of restaurants generated 80% of overall revenue.
- The overall average restaurant rating was approximately 4.34.
- Revenue concentration analysis highlighted the importance of leading restaurant partners.
